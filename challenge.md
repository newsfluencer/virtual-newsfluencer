# Virtual Newsfluencer for Accessible News

## Organization

Fontys University of Applied Sciences

## Background and Description

More than two million Dutch people have low literacy skills or struggle with digital information.
Traditional news platforms often fail to adequately cater to their language proficiency, prior knowledge, and information needs. As a result, they participate less in social discussions and public debate.

Design and develop an interactive Virtual Newsfluencer that translates complex news stories into understandable, personalized, and interactive information. Users should be able to ask questions, receive additional explanations, and be presented with news at an appropriate language level.

The solution uses AI to make news more understandable, accessible, and personalized without compromising the reliability of the content.

## Products

Functional interactive prototype (web or app) / Front-end application
